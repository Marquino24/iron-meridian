# Tech trees

Generated by `node tools/print-tech.mjs` from `js/tech.js`. Every era uses the same 28-tech skeleton (same effects and requirements), with era names. One research slot to start; the tier I Economy tech that gives "+1 research slot" opens a second and the tier III one a third. Up to 5 more techs can wait in the queue.

Cost and time per tier (only the era's resources apply): tier I: 400 money, 120 food, 80 steel, 150 game hours (2.5 real minutes at 1×); tier II: 1500 money, 400 food, 300 steel, 40 oil (+ electronics from the Cold War on), 540 game hours (9.0 real minutes at 1×); tier III: 4000 money, 1000 food, 800 steel, 120 oil (+ electronics from the Cold War on), 1260 game hours (21.0 real minutes at 1×).
