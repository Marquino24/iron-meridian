# Joining the Iron Meridian beta

1. Open **https://marquino24.github.io/iron-meridian/** on a computer or tablet (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
2. Choose how to sign in: Google, Discord, Microsoft, GitHub, Twitch or X. Approve the request on that site and you'll come back to the game.
   - Or type your email and click **Email me a link**, then open the link **in the same browser**.
3. Click **Request beta access**.
4. You'll see **Pending approval**. You can leave the page open (it unlocks by itself the moment you're approved) or come back later.
5. Once approved, pick an **era**, then a **nation**, and click **Assume command**.

Next time you visit, you're signed in automatically. To switch accounts, use **Sign out** at the bottom of the start screen.

If you see **Access not granted**, your request wasn't approved this time.
