# Joining the Iron Meridian beta

1. Open **https://marquino24.github.io/iron-meridian/** on a computer or tablet (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
2. Sign in with **Google**, **Discord**, **GitHub** or **Twitch**. Approve on that site and you'll come back to the game.
   - Or type your email and click **Email me a link**, then open the link **in the same browser**. Email links can be slow or not arrive at all. If yours doesn't come within a few minutes, use Discord or Google instead.
3. You'll see **Pending approval**. That's normal: every new tester is approved by hand. You can leave the page open (it unlocks by itself the moment you're approved) or come back later.
4. Once approved, pick an **era**, then a **nation**, and click **Assume command**.

Next time you visit, you're signed in automatically. To switch accounts, use **Sign out** at the bottom of the main menu.

If you see **Access not granted**, your request wasn't approved this time.
