Privacy

Iron Meridian closed beta · last updated October 2026

What we collect

When you sign in, the sign-in provider you choose (Google, Discord, GitHub, Twitch, or an email link) shares your account ID, display name and email address with us. We store these, plus the date you asked to join the beta and whether your request was approved.

Why

  • To let you sign in and keep you signed in on your device.
  • To decide who can join the closed beta and to recognise you when you come back.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

Accounts and beta requests are stored with Supabase, which hosts our sign-in service and database. Your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage so you don't have to sign in every time; signing out removes it. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who can see it

Only you and the game's developer. Other testers can't see your details.

Deleting your data

Ask the developer to delete your account (for example through the project's GitHub page) and your account and beta request will be removed.
