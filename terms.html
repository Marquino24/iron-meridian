Iron Meridian is an unfinished game in closed testing. Features, maps and saved progress can change or be reset at any time, and the service may be unavailable.

Access

Access is by approval and can be withdrawn at any time. Please don't share your account or try to get around the beta gate.

No warranty

The game is provided as it is, free of charge, without any guarantee.

Map data

Historical borders come from historical-basemaps (GPL-3.0) and modern borders from Natural Earth (public domain). Borders are simplified for gameplay and aren't a statement about any real territorial dispute.
